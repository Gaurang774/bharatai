# Problem Statement Brief

**Gov_01 — One Nation. One AI Framework. Total Trust.**

---

Government employees across Indian ministries and departments are increasingly adopting foreign public AI tools — such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and Google Gemini — for policy drafting, document analysis, summarization, and citizen grievance handling. This shadow adoption, driven by urgent productivity needs, creates a **critical data sovereignty crisis**.

Every query sent to these foreign platforms is routed to servers outside India, resulting in **systemic data leakage** and **untracked exfiltration of state intellectual property**. Sensitive queries related to infrastructure planning, tax policy, defense logistics, and citizen data may be used to train foreign commercial models — directly compromising India's digital sovereignty and competitive edge. There is **no audit trail**, **no ministry-level isolation**, and **no compliance** with Indian data protection mandates.

**BharatAI** solves this by providing a **sovereign, self-hosted AI platform** that runs entirely on Indian infrastructure. It delivers a locally hosted LLM (Llama 3), a government document-grounded RAG engine, a real-time prompt firewall, PII redaction, ministry-isolated data namespaces, and a full governance audit trail — ensuring that **no government data ever leaves Indian borders**.
